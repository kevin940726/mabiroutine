Shared purchase pools

A prototype of one idea: when two merchants sell the same capped stock, the cap is one counter, not one per merchant. Nothing here is the real app. State lives in this page and resets on reload.

The question: does a shared counter read better as one tracker row that names both merchants (model A), or as one row per merchant that share a number (model B)? And does a 共用 marker on the tile make the shared stock obvious before you pin it?
